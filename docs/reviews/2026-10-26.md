## Resume behavior and validation

Exact optimizer resume remains the contract. This change does not introduce a
learning-rate override mode or modify the checkpoint format. A user can omit
`--lr` or supply its stored value; a conflicting explicit value now reports
the stored rate and fails instead of silently ignoring the argument.

The pre-fix reproducer's commands, binary/source hashes and identical output
hashes are in `2026-10-26-resume-lr.json`. Permanent `TrainCli.cmake` coverage
checks rejection without model/checkpoint/metrics artifacts, omitted-rate
byte-identical continuation and existing matching-rate exact resume. The full
CLI workflow test passed in both C17 and C23 builds. This is not a claim that
the unresolved larger-model training parity gate has passed.

## Priority reconciliation

Keep the user's 125% llama.cpp target on both short and long decode, numerical
correctness, C17/C23 ownership and removable optional components intact.
Latest matched selective-FDPP measurements remain 94.70% short / 114.23% long.
